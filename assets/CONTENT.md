## Journal

Edit `journalEntries` in `journal.js`, then redeploy. Each entry has a category, title, and visibility (`soon`, `public`, or `locked`). Public entries can include text and an HTTP(S) URL for a vlog, book, movie, quote, note, photo, or video.

Locked cards are placeholders only. Do not include private text, media, passwords, or destination URLs anywhere in the deployed files. Actual private publishing and an owner dashboard require an authenticated backend; they are not implemented here.
